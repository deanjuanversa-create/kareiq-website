Bystander — Caregiving Support

A trained caregiver to stay with your loved one for half a day, a full day or round the clock.

When your loved one needs someone by their side, a Bystander from the KareIQ family stays with them and supports their everyday needs and comfort, so that the family can work, rest or travel with peace of mind. Choose the length of support that suits the day.
