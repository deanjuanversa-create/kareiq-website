Get to know KareIQ

At KareIQ, we believe ageing should be supported by care, dignity, independence and meaningful connections. Our integrated ecosystem brings together services designed to help older adults and their families navigate ageing with greater confidence.

About KareIQ

What is KareIQ?

KareIQ Healthtech Private Limited is a technology-driven healthcare and elder-care company focused on building an integrated ecosystem to support older adults. Through our services, we aim to make ageing healthier, safer, more engaging and more fulfilling.

What is the vision behind KareIQ?

Our vision is to create an ecosystem where older adults can access the care, support, wellness opportunities and community connections they need to live with dignity and happiness.

Who can benefit from KareIQ's services?

Our services are designed for older adults who wish to maintain their independence, improve their well-being, receive support at home or enjoy a more socially connected lifestyle. We also support families and caregivers seeking reliable solutions for their ageing loved ones.

What makes KareIQ different?

KareIQ takes an integrated approach to elder care. Rather than viewing healthcare, daily support and social engagement as separate needs, we aim to bring these elements together through coordinated services and technology-enabled solutions.

Where does KareIQ operate?

KareIQ is based in Kerala, India. The availability of services depends on the location and operational coverage of each KareIQ service. Please contact us to confirm availability in your area.

Our Services

What services does KareIQ currently offer?

KareIQ currently focuses on two key service brands:

  • Kare@Home: Care and support services delivered at home, helping older adults remain comfortable and supported in familiar surroundings.
  • KareNest: A senior community offering residential living and day-visitor experiences centred on wellness, meaningful activities and social connection.
What is Kare@Home?

Kare@Home brings elder-care services to the comfort of your home. It is designed to support older adults with care and assistance while helping families feel more confident about their loved ones' well-being.

What is KareNest?

KareNest is a senior living and community engagement initiative that offers both residential and day-visitor options. It provides opportunities for older adults to socialize, participate in activities and enjoy a supportive community environment.

How do I choose between Kare@Home and KareNest?

Kare@Home may be suitable for individuals who prefer to remain at home and need support in their familiar environment. KareNest may be suitable for those who wish to experience community living, participate in activities or explore residential accommodation.

Our team can help you understand the available options based on your needs and preferences.

Does KareIQ offer preventive healthcare and wellness programs?

KareIQ is building a wellness-focused approach to ageing that emphasizes prevention, early identification of health risks and healthier lifestyles. The availability of specific programs and services may vary by location and service offering.

Accessing Our Services

How can I enquire about KareIQ services?

You can submit an enquiry through our website or contact the relevant service brand directly. Our team will help you understand the available services, package options and next steps.

Can I enquire on behalf of my parents or an elderly family member?

Absolutely. Family members and caregivers can contact KareIQ on behalf of their loved ones to explore suitable care and community options.

How do I know which service or package is right for my family?

Every individual has different needs and preferences. Our team can help you understand the available services and packages based on your loved one's lifestyle, support requirements and expectations.

Are services available for short-term and long-term needs?

The duration and availability of services depend on the specific offering and package. Please contact Kare@Home or KareNest directly for details about short-term, trial or ongoing arrangements.

How much do KareIQ services cost?

Pricing varies depending on the service, package, duration and individual requirements. Please visit the relevant service website or contact our team for the latest pricing and inclusions.

Care, Safety & Trust

How does KareIQ approach the safety and well-being of older adults?

We believe elder care should be built on trust, respect and dignity. Our services are designed with the needs of older adults in mind, with an emphasis on supportive care, individual preferences and well-being.

Does KareIQ provide medical or emergency services?

KareIQ's services focus on elder care, support and wellness. The scope of healthcare services depends on the specific offering. Emergency medical situations require immediate assistance from local emergency services or the nearest appropriate healthcare facility.

Can KareIQ support individuals with different levels of care needs?

Our services are intended to address a range of ageing-related needs, but suitability depends on the individual's condition and the services available. Please discuss specific care requirements with our team before enrolling.
