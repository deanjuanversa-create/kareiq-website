About KareNest

What is KareNest?

KareNest is a senior living and community engagement initiative by KareIQ, designed to help older adults live healthier, happier and more fulfilling lives. We bring together comfortable living, meaningful activities, social connection and wellness-focused services in a supportive community.

Is KareNest a residential senior living facility or a day-care community?

KareNest offers both residential living and day-visitor options. Older adults can choose to stay with us or visit during the day to participate in activities, socialize and enjoy the community without moving away from their homes.

Who can join KareNest?

KareNest welcomes older adults who wish to enjoy a more active, socially connected and engaging lifestyle. Whether you are looking for a residential option or simply want to spend quality time in a community, we can help you explore a suitable package.

What makes KareNest different?

KareNest goes beyond accommodation. We focus on creating a community where older adults can build friendships, participate in meaningful activities, maintain their well-being and enjoy a sense of belonging—with dignity and independence.

Residential Living

What residential accommodation options are available?

KareNest offers thoughtfully planned living packages designed to provide comfort, convenience and a welcoming community environment. Accommodation options and inclusions vary by package. Please contact us to learn more about the available choices.

What is included in the residential packages?

Depending on the selected package, inclusions may cover accommodation, meals, personalized nutrition planning, preventive wellness services and access to community activities. Our team will explain the package details and inclusions before you make a decision.

Are meals provided at KareNest?

Yes. Residential packages include scheduled meals and refreshments. We also consider individual dietary needs and preferences as part of our personalized nutrition approach, subject to assessment and package inclusions.

Can residents continue their daily routines and personal activities?

Absolutely. KareNest encourages residents to maintain their independence, personal interests and daily routines while enjoying the benefits of community living and organized activities.

Day Visitors & Community Activities

Can I visit KareNest without staying overnight?

Yes! KareNest welcomes day visitors who want to spend time in the community, participate in activities, meet new people and enjoy a meaningful day without opting for residential accommodation.

What activities are offered at KareNest?

Our community activities are designed to encourage social interaction, physical movement, mental engagement, creativity and emotional well-being. Activities may include group interactions, games, recreational sessions, wellness activities and special community events.

Do I need to participate in every activity?

Not at all. Participation is entirely based on individual interests, comfort and ability. There is also plenty of time to relax, socialize or simply enjoy the community at your own pace.

Can family members visit residents?

KareNest values family connections. Family visits can be arranged in accordance with community guidelines and the resident's preferences. Please contact our team for visiting arrangements.

Health & Well-being

Does KareNest provide healthcare services?

KareNest incorporates preventive health and wellness into its community approach. Depending on the package and services available, this may include wellness assessments, health monitoring and access to relevant healthcare support coordinated through KareIQ.

Are personalized wellness programs available?

Yes. KareNest offers access to wellness-focused programs designed to support areas such as mobility, emotional well-being and healthy ageing. Our team can help you understand which programs are currently available and suitable for your needs.

Is KareNest suitable for individuals who need intensive medical care?

KareNest is designed primarily for older adults seeking community living, engagement and wellness support. Individuals requiring intensive medical supervision or specialized nursing care may need a different care setting. We recommend discussing individual care needs with our team before joining.

Packages & Joining

What packages does KareNest offer?

KareNest offers residential living packages as well as options for day visitors. Package inclusions, duration and pricing vary. Contact us to receive the latest package details and identify the option that best suits your needs.

Can I experience KareNest before joining?

Yes! Your first day at KareNest is on us. We offer a complimentary day pass so you can experience the community, explore the facilities, meet our team and discover the activities before making a decision.

How do I enroll myself or a family member?

Simply contact our team or submit an enquiry through our website. We’ll understand your preferences, explain the available packages, arrange a visit and guide you through the joining process.

Can I join KareNest for a short period?

Please contact our team to discuss your preferred duration and the available residential or day-visitor options. We’ll help you explore the arrangements that best suit your needs.

How can I get more information about KareNest?

You can reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. Our team will be happy to answer your questions, arrange a visit and help you discover life at KareNest.
