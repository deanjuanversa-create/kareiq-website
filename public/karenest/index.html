KareBalance

Better Balance.Stronger Steps.Fewer Falls.

A personalised fall prevention and mobility programme that helps older adults stay steady, active, and independent.

See programmes & pricing

How KareBalance Helps

  • Improve Balance & Stability

    Build better control while standing, turning and walking.

  • Build Strength

    Strengthen leg and core muscles for everyday activities.

  • Move with Confidence

    Practise walking, stair climbing and sit-to-stand transitions.

  • Identify Fall Risks

    Assess personal risk factors, medication-related concerns and home safety hazards.

  • Stay Independent

    Develop safer movement habits and confidence in daily life.

Who Is KareBalance For?

KareBalance is designed for older adults who:

  • Have experienced a fall or near-fall.
  • Fear falling and have started avoiding everyday activities.
  • Feel unsteady while walking, turning or climbing stairs.
  • Are recovering mobility after an illness, hospitalisation or a period of inactivity.
  • Have reduced leg strength, balance or mobility.
  • Want to maintain their balance, strength and independence as they age.

Our Programmes & Pricing

Choose the right level of support for your needs and goals.

KareBalance Base

Assessment & Report

One-time fee

What's included

  • Comprehensive fall risk assessment
  • Balance, strength and mobility testing
  • Review of fall history and relevant risk factors
  • Home safety recommendations
  • Personalised assessment report with next steps

Best for: Understanding individual fall risks and prevention needs.

KareBalance Plus

Structured Training & Prevention

₹39,999

24 supervised training sessions

What's included

  • Initial fall risk assessment
  • 24 supervised training sessions
  • Progressive balance and strength exercises
  • Walking, turning and sit-to-stand practice
  • Home safety review
  • Progress monitoring and family report

Best for: Older adults with balance difficulties, reduced strength or a history of falls.

KareBalance Premium

Comprehensive Six-Month Support

₹90,000

Six-month programme

What's included

  • Comprehensive initial assessment
  • Weekly supervised balance and strength training
  • Quarterly reassessments
  • Home visits and safety recommendations
  • Family coaching and caregiver guidance
  • Ongoing progress tracking and personalised programme adjustments

Best for: Older adults needing continued support to maintain mobility, confidence and independence.

Not sure which programme is right for your loved one?

Our team can help identify the most appropriate level of support based on mobility, fall history and individual goals.

Note: Final pricing may vary based on assessment complexity, session frequency, home-visit requirements and service location. Clinical suitability should be established before beginning supervised training. KareBalance is a fall risk reduction and mobility support programme and does not guarantee the prevention of falls.
