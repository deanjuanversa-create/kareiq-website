About KareIQ

Reimagining Ageing. Redefining Elder Care.

Building a more connected, compassionate and technology-driven future for ageing.

At KareIQ, we believe that growing older should never mean compromising on health, independence, dignity or happiness.

As families evolve and the needs of our ageing population become more complex, eldercare needs a new approach: one that brings together compassionate human care, meaningful social connections and the power of technology.

KareIQ is building a technology-driven, integrated eldercare ecosystem designed to make quality care more accessible, personalised and connected. Our vision is to create an environment where older adults can live with confidence, remain meaningfully engaged and enjoy a better quality of life, while their families experience greater peace of mind.

Our Vision

To revolutionize healthcare by creating a seamless, personalized, and proactive care ecosystem that empowers individuals to live healthier, longer, and more independent lives.

Our Mission

To deliver intelligent, tech-enabled care that empowers individuals with personalized health support through every stage of life.

Our Approach

Technology with a Human Touch

We believe technology should make care more human, not less.

At KareIQ, we are working towards a more connected approach to eldercare, combining the warmth of personal relationships with the efficiency, accessibility and consistency that technology can enable. Our approach is guided by three principles:

  1. 01

    Care with Dignity

    Every individual deserves to be treated with respect, empathy and understanding. We put the needs, preferences and independence of older adults at the centre of our thinking.

  2. 02

    Connected Experiences

    Eldercare is not a single service or a one-time intervention. It is an ongoing journey that involves individuals, families, caregivers and communities. We aim to make these experiences more seamless and connected.

  3. 03

    Technology for Better Living

    We see technology as an enabler of more accessible, coordinated and personalised care, helping families navigate eldercare with greater confidence and convenience.

Why KareIQ?

Choosing eldercare is deeply personal.

It involves trust, emotional reassurance and the confidence that loved ones are being cared for with genuine concern.

That is why we are building KareIQ around more than services. We are building a philosophy of care that values independence, strengthens family confidence and recognises the importance of physical, emotional and social well-being.

By combining human empathy with technology-led thinking, we aspire to make the eldercare experience simpler, more dependable and more meaningful for everyone involved.

Vinaya Ulhas Pai

Founder, CEO

Vinaya Ulhas Pai is the Founder and CEO of KareIQ. She is focussed on building an integrated eldercare ecosystem to improve the health, dignity, independence, and happiness of older adults.

With extensive experience in social work, public health, programme management, and development initiatives across non-profit and government organisations, Vinaya brings expertise in operations and community health education.
