About KareWellness

Healthy ageing is more than care. It is staying well.

KareWellness is where KareIQ's preventive and wellness programmes come together, so that support for balance, mobility and emotional wellbeing continues wherever care happens: at home or in a residential community.

  • Preventive healthcare

    Assessments that find risks early, before they become problems.

  • Healthy ageing

    Habits, routines and activity that help older adults stay strong and engaged.

  • Fall prevention & mobility

    Balance, strength and safer movement with KareBalance.

  • Mental wellness

    Emotional wellbeing, stress, sleep and connection with KareMind.

  • Personalised support

    Every programme starts with an assessment and a plan for the person.

  • Continuity of care

    The same programmes and progress, whether at home or in residential care.
