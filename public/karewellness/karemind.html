KareMind · A structured mental wellness program supporting emotional wellbeing, stress management, healthy sleep, resilience and social connection.

Mental wellbeing is an essential part of healthy ageing. At KareMind, we believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to feel emotionally balanced, stay socially connected, and navigate life's challenges with confidence and dignity.

KareMind is a structured mental wellness program designed to support emotional wellbeing, stress management, healthy sleep habits, resilience, and meaningful social connections. Through personalised assessments, one-to-one support from qualified professionals, and practical strategies for everyday life, the program helps individuals better understand their emotional needs and build healthier routines.

Whether you are experiencing everyday stress, adjusting to life changes, feeling socially disconnected, or simply looking to prioritise your mental wellbeing, KareMind offers support tailored to your individual needs.

Our approach is personal, compassionate, and focused on you.

With ongoing guidance, progress reviews, and referrals to appropriate mental health professionals when needed, KareMind helps you take meaningful steps towards a healthier, more balanced life.

Objectives & benefits

Support for the parts of mental wellbeing that matter most in later life.

  • Emotional wellbeing

    Understand emotional needs and feel more balanced day to day.

  • Stress management

    Practical coping strategies for everyday stress and life changes.

  • Healthy sleep

    Guidance on sleep routines, relaxation and healthy daily habits.

  • Resilience

    Build coping skills, confidence and healthier routines.

  • Social connection

    Support for staying connected and adjusting to life changes.

Services & activities

  • Structured mental wellness and lifestyle assessment
  • Validated screening questionnaires, administered by trained professionals
  • Individual counselling or wellbeing sessions with qualified professionals
  • Personalised stress-management and coping plan
  • Guidance on sleep routines, relaxation and healthy habits
  • Progress reviews and reassessments
  • Family education session, with participant consent
  • Referral coordination for psychological or psychiatric care when indicated

Who is KareMind for?

Whether you are experiencing everyday stress, adjusting to life changes, feeling socially disconnected, or simply looking to prioritise your mental wellbeing:

  • Base: An initial assessment and a clear plan for improving wellbeing.
  • Plus: People who would benefit from regular professional support and guided habit changes.
  • Premium: People seeking sustained support, continuity and a longer-term wellbeing plan.

Programme features

  • Assessment first

    Every program begins with a structured assessment and a written action plan.

  • One-to-one support

    Sessions with appropriately qualified professionals, matched to assessed needs.

  • Measured progress

    Progress reviews and reassessments show what is changing.

  • Connected care

    Referrals to a clinical psychologist or psychiatrist when further care is indicated.
