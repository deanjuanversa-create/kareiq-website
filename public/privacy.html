1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how KareIQ Healthtech Private Limited (“KareIQ”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information provided by users (“you”, “your”) through our website kareiq.co.in (“Website”) and our mobile application for elder services (“KareIQ App”). Together, these platforms and their content are referred to as “KareIQ Services”.

By accessing or using KareIQ Services, you acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Personal Information We Collect

You may choose not to provide personally identifiable information. However, if you use KareIQ Services or share data with us, it is done voluntarily and at your own discretion.

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Name, address, email ID, mobile number, age

  • IP address and device identifiers

  • Payment details

  • Any other information you provide while using KareIQ Services

When using the KareIQ App, we may access your device’s Firebase ID or other technical identifiers to store preferences and monitor usage for service improvement.

Participation in additional schemes or services may require further personal details, which will be treated as confidential and stored securely.

3. Use of Personal Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide and improve our services

  • Analyze usage patterns and enhance user experience

  • Communicate offers, newsletters, product launches, and updates

  • Comply with legal obligations or respond to government or court requests

We may share your information with trusted sponsors, service providers, and business partners on a need-to-know basis. Aggregated data may be used for analysis and shared accordingly.

4. Cookies

KareIQ may use cookies—small text files stored on your device—to:

  • Remember your preferences

  • Enhance your experience with KareIQ Services

Cookies do not contain personal information but may link to data you have knowingly provided.

5. Advertisements

KareIQ Services may display advertisements from third-party advertisers. These advertisers may use device identifiers. KareIQ does not endorse or guarantee the quality, reliability, or privacy practices of these advertisers. You engage with them at your own risk.

6. Data Security

While KareIQ takes reasonable precautions aligned with industry standards to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security due to potential risks such as hacking or malware. We continuously strive to safeguard your information.

7. Third-Party Sites

KareIQ Services may contain links to external websites not operated by KareIQ. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

KareIQ reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. We will continue to use your personal information in accordance with the policy in effect at the time of collection. Please check this page periodically for updates. Continued use of KareIQ Services constitutes your acceptance of any changes.
