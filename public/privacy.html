Privacy Policy

Applies to KareIQ websites, including Kare@Home and KareNest

Draft for review: have this policy checked against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 by your legal advisor before launch.

What we collect

When you book a service or send an enquiry, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, mobile number, email (if given), area and city, the service or package you chose, your preferred visit time and any notes about health needs.

How we use it

We use these details only to contact you about your request, plan care, schedule visits and send updates you ask for. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Health information

Notes about health conditions are seen only by the care team members who need them to plan and deliver care.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to care are deleted after 12 months. Records of care provided are kept as long as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time by calling or messaging +91 81293 45178.
